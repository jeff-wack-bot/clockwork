Clockwork Grid

    
Regions are registers. A region is a set of painted cells holding one number, and its colour shows the value. Blocks are chips: on every tick each chip reads the regions on its input pads and writes the region on its output pad, and all regions change at once.

    
Unwritten regions are constants. Give one a handle with the knob tool and turn it. A written region can't have a handle.

    
Every block is a pipeline stage. A feedback loop through k blocks takes k ticks to go round. The right-hand panel lists every loop's latency.

    
Crossings need the bottom layer. Regions can't overlap each other, only pads. Press Tab while painting to drop a via and continue on the bottom layer, which passes under everything. Tab again to come back up.

    
Zoom is navigation. Scroll into a composite block to see inside it. Once its cells are big enough you are editing inside it: the definition is shared, but each instance keeps its own knob values. Double-click a block to fly into it, Z or Esc to back out, H to see everything.

    
One clock. The slider runs the design from 0.5 ticks/s up to real time (48 000 ticks/s, one per audio sample). With audio on, the speaker holds the DAC register between ticks, so slow clocks are clicks and real time is sound.
